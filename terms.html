About the app

Ikasi & Go™ is a Basque vocabulary learning app for iOS. It provides vocabulary flashcards, spaced repetition quizzes, and cultural notes to help users learn Basque (Euskara).

Free and Pro access

The app offers a free tier that includes the beginner (A1) vocabulary across all topics, with quizzes, spaced repetition, and daily goals. Ikasi Pro is a one-time lifetime purchase that unlocks everything: all levels (A1 to B2), every topic, every game, and all future vocabulary updates. There is no subscription and there are no recurring charges.

  • Free trial: 7 days of full Pro access at no charge. The trial does not convert into a purchase automatically, and no payment method is charged when it ends; you simply return to the free tier.
  • Lifetime unlock: a single one-time purchase, yours permanently.

Purchases are processed by Apple and are subject to Apple's terms and conditions. Refund requests are handled by Apple through their standard process at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What the lifetime unlock covers

It is permanent. Your purchase unlocks all levels (A1 to B2), every topic, and every game in the app, and it keeps working for as long as you have the app. It will never expire, and it will never turn into a subscription.

Vocabulary updates are included, permanently. New words and phrases, corrections, and improved notes reach you at no additional cost, forever. This is the core of what you are buying and we will not put it behind a further charge.

Improvements to the app are included. New quiz modes, new games, redesigns, and fixes are part of the app you bought, not a separate purchase.

What could cost extra later. Some things would cost us money every month, for every person using them: syncing your progress through a server, hosted audio recordings, or anything that depends on a paid third-party service. A single payment cannot fund a monthly cost forever. If we ever build something like that, it will be entirely optional, we will say plainly what it costs before you pay anything, and we will never take an existing feature and move it behind that charge. Declining it changes nothing about what you already own: the app keeps working exactly as it does today, and the vocabulary updates keep arriving.
