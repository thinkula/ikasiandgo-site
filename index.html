Etxea
House / Home
eh-CHEH-ah

For centuries the etxea was the basic unit of Basque law and life, and in Bizkaia ancestral houses hold special legal status to this day. Families took their surnames from their houses, and houses were named for the land around them.

Nire etxea mendian dago.
My house is on the mountain.
